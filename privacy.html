Privacy policy

Open Board, effective 21 November 2026

Open Board is a chess app published by AHUMUZA FITLIFE SOLUTIONS, a business registered with the Uganda Registration Services Bureau (URSB) ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Open Board does not ask you to create an account, and it does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.

What stays on your device

The app saves a few things in your device's own storage so it can work the way you left it: your settings, your chosen computer level, an unfinished game, which puzzles you solved, your ratings, statistics, achievements and daily puzzle streak, and, if you play online, your anonymous account ID. This information does not leave your device unless you play online, as described below. Clearing the app's data removes it.

The puzzles, sounds and chess engine are built into the app. Playing against the computer, solving puzzles and reviewing your games happen entirely on your device.

Online games

When you open online play, the app signs in to Google Firebase Authentication anonymously. This creates a random account ID for the app on your device. It is not linked to your name, email address, phone number or any other account.

When you play online, the app sends the following to our game server so both phones stay in sync: the 5-character game code, the moves played, the game clock if you chose one, draw offers and the result, the time the game was created or updated, and your anonymous account ID. The game server is a Google Firebase Realtime Database. Google processes this data, and the usual connection information such as your IP address, on our behalf under its own terms and privacy policy.

Quick match: while you look for an opponent, your anonymous account ID, the clock you chose and the time you started looking are kept in a waiting list so another player can be matched with you. Your entry is removed when you are matched or stop looking, and any left behind are deleted after a few minutes.

Anyone who has your game code can open that game and see the moves. Do not share the code with people you do not want to play.

Games are deleted automatically once nobody has played them for 7 days, and may be deleted sooner.

Fonts

The app loads its text fonts from Google Fonts when you are online. When it does, Google receives the usual information any website receives, such as your IP address and device type.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics or tracking tools.
  • No sale of data, and no sharing of data except with Google as the service provider described above.

Children

The app is not aimed at children under 13. It does not ask for or knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Data on your phone: clear Open Board's storage in your phone settings, or uninstall the app. This removes your settings, progress, ratings and your anonymous account ID from the phone.

Online games: games are deleted automatically once nobody has played them for 7 days.

To have your online data deleted sooner, including your anonymous account ID: email ahumuzamark21213@gmail.com with the subject "Open Board data deletion" and include a game code you played. We delete the games and the account ID within 30 days and confirm by email. We keep no copy afterwards.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
